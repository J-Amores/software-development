# Modern Admin Dashboard Domain Glossary

Core business terminology for the administrative dashboard and operational management platform.

## Organizational Roles & Access

**Operator**:
An authorized administrative individual responsible for configuring the system, managing access privileges, and overseeing organizational operations.
_Avoid_: Superuser, admin user, root, account owner.

**Team Member**:
An internal employee or collaborator belonging to the organization who participates in organizational workflows.
_Avoid_: Staff, employee, agent, personnel, coworker.

**Access Level**:
A designated tier of organizational authority (Admin, Manager, User) that governs permitted administrative actions and functional responsibilities.
_Avoid_: Role, permission group, privilege tier.

## Relationships & Communications

**Contact**:
An external individual, client representative, or institutional contact with whom the organization maintains an operational or commercial relationship.
_Avoid_: Lead, prospect, client profile, customer record.

**FAQ Entry**:
A curated paired question and authorized administrative answer addressing common organizational guidelines or institutional inquiries.
_Avoid_: Knowledge base article, help ticket, Q&A item.

## Commercial & Financial Operations

**Invoice**:
A formal, itemized request for payment issued by the organization to a client for delivered services or products.
_Avoid_: Bill, payment request, tab.

**Amount**:
The quantified monetary value billed on an invoice or settled in a commercial transaction.
_Avoid_: Cost, price, fee, charge, total due.

**Transaction**:
A recorded financial exchange or settlement of monetary value associated with an invoice or commercial activity.
_Avoid_: Payment, charge, transfer, remittance.

## Planning & Analytics

**Calendar Event**:
A scheduled operational milestone, meeting, or appointment anchored to a specific date, date range, or designated time on the organizational schedule.
_Avoid_: Booking, schedule item, reminder.

**Campaign Metric**:
A quantified performance indicator measuring the reach, financial yield, or operational performance of an organizational initiative.
_Avoid_: Statistic, KPI, analytics point, measurement.

**Operational Report**:
A consolidated formal summary extract of organizational metrics, commercial transactions, and administrative performance compiled for executive review or audit.
_Avoid_: Export file, download, CSV dump, data dump, spreadsheet.
